NEXT

GA release · Weeks 5–8
02
B1Q1 · High value / lower effort

One-Click Compliance Checklist

Tackles a significant 14.6-minute workflow bottleneck and reduces administrative effort, particularly for Dispatchers, but compliance is not the clearest expression of either persona’s primary moment of misery.

B6Q2 · High value / higher effort

Driver Alert Notifications

Directly addresses the 8–15-minute route-change communication delay and connects Dispatcher decisions with Driver execution; integration and offline reliability need validation. This is key to address the core requirement that we need to fix.

Validate integration, delivery reliability and offline behaviour before GA commitment.

LATER

Backlog · Reassess as evidence evolves
03
B5Q3 · Lower value / lower effort

Step Progress Indicator

Easy to implement, but showing workflow progress does little to remove the underlying steps, delays or manual workarounds.

B9Q3 · Lower value / lower effort

Compliance Audit Trail Export

Useful for audit and compliance needs, but it is a downstream administrative benefit rather than a meaningful reduction in daily Driver or Dispatcher workarounds.

B10Q4 · Lower value / higher effort

In-App Coordinator Training

May help new Dispatchers learn the product, but training does not fix a workflow that is inherently slow, complex or unreliable.

Keep visible in backlog; do not displace work that removes operational friction.
