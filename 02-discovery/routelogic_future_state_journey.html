RouteLogic · Product initiative

Future-State Customer Journey

A four-stage operational journey that moves from dispatch to delivery and back to dispatch visibility—replacing parallel tools, manual workarounds and duplicated communication.

Strategic ambition Reclaim operational simplicity by making RouteLogic the fastest, most reliable way for drivers and dispatchers to manage everyday fleet operations.

Value proposition For frontline fleet coordinators, deliver a faster, simpler operational experience that makes daily fleet activities effortless—protecting customer retention and RouteLogic’s market position.

Future-state journey · Four connected stages

STAGE 01

Assign & synchronise

Fleet Dispatcher

User Action
Reassign route → Driver receives the updated instructions promptly.

Internal State
Confident that drivers are working from the current plan.
Pain Point Addressed
8–15-minute route-update delays and WhatsApp coordination.
STAGE 02

Start & navigate

Frontline Driver

User Action
Open route → Access the stop list, including when connectivity drops.

Internal State
Prepared and confident the route remains available throughout the shift.
Pain Point Addressed
Missing offline stop lists, route loss after crashes and paper backups.
STAGE 03

Complete & confirm

Frontline Driver

User Action
Complete stop → Record delivery and proof with minimal steps.

Internal State
Uninterrupted and confident the delivery record has been saved.
Pain Point Addressed
Three-screen completion, failed photo uploads and manual workarounds.
STAGE 04

Monitor & act

Fleet Dispatcher

User Action
View delivery status → Identify exceptions and respond using current information.

Internal State
In control and able to trust the operational dashboard.
Pain Point Addressed
20–60-minute status delays and manual driver verification.
Operational feedback from monitoring informs the next round of route assignment and coordination.

Three competitive advantages over manual workarounds

01 · ONE CONNECTED RECORD

One operational record

Route changes, delivery outcomes and proof of delivery stay together, reducing duplicated communication.

02 · RELIABLE FIELD ACCESS

Reliable access in the field

Drivers can access route information when connectivity is unavailable, reducing dependence on screenshots and paper manifests.

03 · TIMELY VISIBILITY

Timely operational visibility

Dispatchers can monitor delivery progress in RouteLogic instead of chasing updates through WhatsApp and phone calls.

Strategic outcome

For driversLess administration, fewer interruptions and faster delivery completion.
For dispatchersMore reliable coordination, current information and greater operational control.
For the businessStronger frontline adoption, greater enterprise value realisation and reduced customer retention risk.
Success means RouteLogic becomes the easiest and most dependable way to complete and coordinate daily delivery work—so users no longer need a parallel operating system.
